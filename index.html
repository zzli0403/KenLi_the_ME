About Me

I have been drawn to mechanical systems from an early age, fascinated by how engines, linkages, and mechanisms transform simple inputs into controlled motion. That curiosity evolved into hands-on experience when I founded my junior high’s first robotics club, leading a VEX Robotics team through multiple competitive seasons and international events.

At the University of Washington, I am pursuing a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (GPA 3.59), serving as a teaching assistant for ME 123 while developing strong foundations in mechanics, design, manufacturing, and mechatronics. My internships at Bosch and Yanjan further grounded my academic work in real-world engineering environments, where reliability, documentation, and cross-disciplinary collaboration are critical.

I enjoy projects that sit at the intersection of mechanical design and system integration, where thoughtful engineering decisions directly impact performance, robustness, and user experience.
